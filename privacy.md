# NAX Bot — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

## 1. Information We Process
NAX Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, message content, attachments, moderation records, economy balances, experience points, and server settings when required for its features.

## 2. How We Use Information
Information is used to provide moderation, security, logging, economy, leveling, ticket, and server management features.

## 3. Message Logging
When message logging is enabled, deleted messages and available attachments may be recorded for moderation and security purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention
Information may be stored to maintain bot features and server records. Retention depends on the feature and server configuration.

## 5. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal information. Authorized server administrators may access relevant information for moderation and bot functionality.

## 6. Data Requests
For questions about personal data or deletion requests, contact the NAX Bot team through its official support server.

## 7. Policy Updates
This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

By using NAX Bot, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
